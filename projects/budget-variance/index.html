    About this analysis

    Business question: Is revenue on track against budget, and if not, which stream and which months explain the gap?

    Approach: Monthly budget and actual figures for each currency were combined in Power Query into one fact table, linked to a date dimension (star schema). DAX measures calculate variance, variance %, achievement rate and year-to-date totals (DATESYTD), so every visual responds to the currency, month and stream filters.

    What this view adds: a variance bridge that splits the total gap by stream, a month × stream variance heat map, and automatic findings that update with the filters.
