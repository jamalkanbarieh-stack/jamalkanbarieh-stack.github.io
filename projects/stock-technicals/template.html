⚑
Data notes: public DSE daily prices in SYP, not adjusted for corporate actions. The DSE was closed from 8 Dec 2024 to 2 Jun 2025 (shaded on the chart). Two one-day moves beyond the exchange's ±5% limit (8 Feb 2023: −26%; 3 Apr 2024: −21%) coincide with capital increases, most likely bonus-share adjustments. They are marked ◆ and excluded from return, volatility and drawdown statistics. DSE closing prices are reference prices and can sit outside the day's traded high–low range.

    About this analysis

    Question: What do trend, momentum and risk indicators say about BBSF's price behaviour through Syria's 2023–2025 currency collapse, exchange closure and reopening?

    Approach: Daily prices were imported from the exchange's Arabic-language export, with headers translated and types fixed in Power Query. Indicators were built as DAX calculated columns: previous close, daily and log returns, 30-day volatility, 20/50/200-day SMAs, and gains, losses and RSI-14 with an overbought/neutral/oversold status. A candlestick custom visual shows price action.

    What this view adds: Wilder-smoothed RSI (the industry standard), drawdown analysis, a return histogram with fat-tail statistics, golden/death-cross detection, and handling of the exchange closure and corporate-action gaps.
